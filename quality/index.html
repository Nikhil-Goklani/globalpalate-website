Frequently asked questions

1. Can I get a Certificate of Analysis before ordering?

Yes. Request the documentation packet and we will email current specification sheets and sample COAs before you ask for a quote.

2. Which certifications apply to which products?

Certifications vary by ingredient. Each product page lists its purity statements, and the documentation packet includes certificate copies. Tell us which products you are evaluating and we will confirm what applies, including the certifier and certificate details.

3. What is tested on each batch?

Every batch is tested for heavy metals, microbiological safety and pesticide residue, and the results are compiled into that batch's Certificate of Analysis.

4. What if a delivered lot does not match its COA?

Contact us right away with the batch number. We will review it with you.
