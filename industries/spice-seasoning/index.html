Bulk organic spices for blenders and seasoning manufacturers

How we support Spice & Seasoning

Spice and seasoning manufacturers need bulk inputs with predictable color and flavor, and the flexibility to test a new blend before committing to a large purchase.

Global Palate holds organic turmeric, ginger and tamarind in a U.S. warehouse with a 100 kg minimum on powders, which suits blend trials.

Compliance notes

Spice buyers often ask about microbial-reduction treatments and irradiation status. Ask us for the statement that applies to the lot you are buying.

Organic seasoning blends depend on certified-organic ingredients throughout.

What buyers in this segment ask for

  • Consistent color, aroma and particle size across lots.
  • Statements on irradiation and other treatments for the specific lot, which we provide from our documentation.
  • Microbiological results on the batch COA.
  • Reorders through a single point of contact.
  • Bulk packaging suited to blending operations.
