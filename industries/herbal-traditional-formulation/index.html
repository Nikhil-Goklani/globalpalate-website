Traceable organic botanicals for herbal and traditional formulators

How we support Herbal & Traditional Formulation

Herbal and traditional formulators work from a precise botanical definition: the species, the plant part and the form. We state all three for every ingredient we sell.

Each ingredient is a single botanical with no blending, supplied with a batch Certificate of Analysis and organic documentation.

Compliance notes

Describe each ingredient by its botanical name and plant part on your specifications and labels, and keep supplier COAs with each lot.

Finished-product claims are governed by the rules that apply to your product category and market.

What buyers in this segment ask for

  • The Latin name and plant part, such as root, leaf or rhizome.
  • Confirmation that the powder is a single botanical, free from adulteration and additives.
  • Batch traceability and a COA you can keep on file.
  • Organic certification paperwork.
  • Reliable repeat supply from a domestic warehouse.
