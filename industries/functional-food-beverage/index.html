Industry

Organic ingredients for functional food and beverage makers

Manufacturers building turmeric lattes, wellness drinks and functional snack lines.

How we support Functional Food & Beverage

Beverage and food brands need ingredients that behave the same way on every production run: consistent color, flavor and particle size, with paperwork their quality team can file.

Global Palate holds organic turmeric, moringa, ginger, tamarind and arrowroot in a U.S. warehouse, so new-product development does not wait on an import.

Compliance notes

Facilities covered by FDA's preventive controls rule (21 CFR Part 117) must manage supplier approval and verification. Our COAs and certificates can support your supplier file.

Products labeled organic must be made with certified-organic ingredients. Ask us for the organic certificate for the lots you purchase.

What buyers in this segment ask for

  • Color and flavor consistency from lot to lot.
  • Particle size suited to your application, such as a fine powder for liquid blends.
  • An allergen statement and confirmation of non-GMO status.
  • Supplier documentation for your supply-chain program.
  • A small first order to test a formulation before scaling.

Frequently asked questions

1. Which ingredients suit beverage formulations?

Turmeric, moringa and ginger powders are commonly used in beverages, and tamarind paste in concentrates. Tell us your application and we will suggest what to test.

2. Can I order samples?

Yes. Request a sample through the quote form and tell us which ingredient and application you have in mind.

3. Are your ingredients allergen free?

The ingredients in our catalog are free from the major allergens. The allergen statement is part of each product's specification.
