About Global Palate

We supply certified organic ingredients to manufacturers, from U.S. stock.

Global Palate is a U.S.-based business-to-business supplier serving the United States and Canada.

What we do

We hold certified organic ingredients in a warehouse in Compton, California and sell them in bulk to manufacturers. Our catalog today is turmeric, ashwagandha, moringa, ginger, tamarind and arrowroot, and we plan to add more.

Buyers come to us so that a trial batch or a reorder does not depend on an ocean shipment. Every batch is tested and ships with a Certificate of Analysis, and the documentation packet is available before you order.

Who we are

Global Palate is a North America division of Agronic Food Pvt. Ltd.. Our warehouse is at 3040 E. Ana Street, Compton, CA 90221, and our office is at 21 Meridian Rd, Edison, NJ 08820.

We work with 4 types of manufacturer: dietary supplement, functional food and beverage, herbal and traditional formulation, and spice and seasoning.

Operating since 2025. Parent Company in this business from last 17 years.
