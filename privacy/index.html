Information we collect

When you submit a form on this site we collect the details you enter, such as your name, work email, phone number, company, product interest, quantity, delivery ZIP code and message. We also receive technical data such as your browser type and the page you submitted from. If analytics is enabled and you accept cookies, we collect usage data about how the site is used.

We use your information to respond to your request, send documents you ask for, provide quotes and samples, and manage our business relationship with you. We store contact records in our customer relationship management (CRM) system.
