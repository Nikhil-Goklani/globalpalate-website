Frequently asked questions

1. Where does East Coast turmeric ship from?

Inventory is held in our Compton, California warehouse and ships by LTL or full-truckload freight. Our Edison, NJ office handles sales support for East Coast buyers.

2. Is there stock in New Jersey?

No. Our stock is held in Compton, California. The Edison, NJ location is our office.

3. How do I compare this with importing turmeric through an East Coast port?

Importing to order brings ocean freight, customs clearance and container-scale lot sizes. Buying from our stock removes those steps, and cross-country freight cost and timing are confirmed on your quote so you can compare.

4. What is the minimum order?

The minimum order is 100 kg.

5. Can I get the COA before ordering?

Yes. Request the batch documents on the product page or contact us and we will send the Certificate of Analysis and specification sheet.
