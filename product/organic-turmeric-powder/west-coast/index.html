California compliance notes

California buyers often ask about Proposition 65 and heavy metals. Our batches are tested for heavy metals and the results are reported on each Certificate of Analysis, so your team can review them against its own Prop 65 assessment. We supply ingredients; warning and labeling decisions for finished products belong to the manufacturer.

Frequently asked questions

1. Where does West Coast turmeric ship from?

Every order ships from our warehouse at 3040 E. Ana Street, Compton, CA 90221.

2. Which states do you deliver to from Compton?

We deliver across the United States. For California, Oregon, Washington, Nevada, Arizona, Utah and Idaho, freight moves a shorter distance than it would from an East Coast or import-to-order source. Freight options and lead time are confirmed on your quote.

3. Does Proposition 65 apply to turmeric powder?

Proposition 65 requires warnings when a product exposes consumers to listed chemicals above safe-harbor levels. Lead and cadmium are on the list and can occur naturally in botanical raw materials. Review the heavy-metal results on the batch COA as part of your own assessment. Warning decisions for finished products rest with the brand owner.

4. What is the minimum order?

The minimum order is 100 kg.

5. Can I get the COA before ordering?

Yes. Request the batch documents on the product page or contact us and we will send the Certificate of Analysis and specification sheet.
