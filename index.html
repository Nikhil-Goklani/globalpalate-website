Why manufacturers buy from stock instead of importing

Importing direct means ocean freight, customs clearance and lot sizes built for containers. Buying from a U.S. warehouse removes those steps from your production schedule.

Held in the U.S.

Inventory is held in our warehouse in Compton, California, so orders ship domestically instead of waiting on an import.

Documented batches

Every batch is tested and ships with a Certificate of Analysis. Request the documentation packet before you place an order.

Practical minimums

Powders have a 100 kg minimum, which suits pilot batches and new-product trials. Tamarind paste is sold by the drum.

One team

Quotes, documents and reorders go through the same sales team, by phone, email or the quote form.
